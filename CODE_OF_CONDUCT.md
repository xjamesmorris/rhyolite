# Code of Conduct

This project aims to provide a respectful, inclusive, and constructive
environment for everyone who participates.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in feedback, reviews, and disagreement.
- Focus on technical issues, evidence, and actionable next steps.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harm directly.
- Protect privacy and avoid sharing credentials, secrets, or non-public
  repository details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing, threats, or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that makes collaboration unsafe or unproductive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other project spaces managed by the maintainers.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[jmorris@namei.org](mailto:jmorris@namei.org).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or restrict participation in content or
interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.
